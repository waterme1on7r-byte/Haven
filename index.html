One player. Four moods.

Everything for listening. Nothing else.

Waveform seeking

Built from the audio itself at 100 samples per second. Played audio is grey, what's coming is white. Click or drag to scrub.

Drop it in

Drag files or whole folders onto the player to replace the queue. Hold Shift while dropping to add to it instead.

A queue that follows you

The playlist attaches below the player and moves with it. Shuffle, repeat all, repeat one, scroll long queues, clear in one click.

Three sizes, any corner

Small, Medium, or Big. Pin it Always on top, lock its position, and Haven remembers where you left it.

Gets out of the way

Dims to 50% when you click elsewhere and returns to full opacity when you come back. Only one Haven runs at a time.
